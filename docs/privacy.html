Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Claud Clipper ("the app") is a personal tool that uploads edited Twitch clips to the operator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. This policy explains what data it handles.

1. Data we access

  • TikTok: when you sign in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and refresh token and your basic profile information (scope user.info.basic). It uses the video.upload and video.publish scopes only to upload videos to your account and check their upload status.
  • Twitch: public clip information (title, view count, streamer name, clip video) for approved streamers, through the Twitch API.

2. How it is used

Tokens are used only to upload videos to the account that signed in. The app does not read your followers, messages, other videos or any data about other TikTok users.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens are stored in a file on the operator's own computer. They are not sent to any server run by the app, sold or shared with anyone. Video files are processed locally on the same computer.

4. Third parties

Data is exchanged only with the platforms the app connects to: TikTok, YouTube (Google) and Twitch, under their own privacy policies. If AI-written captions are turned on, a clip's transcript text is sent to Anthropic's API to write a short hook line.

5. Deleting your data

Revoke the app's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. The stored token stops working immediately and is deleted from the operator's computer on request.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Contact

Open an issue at github.com/Ethmaster400/Twitch-Clipper/issues.
