These terms cover your use of Claud Clipper ("the app"), a tool run by its operator (GitHub user Ethmaster400) to upload edited Twitch clips to the operator's own social media accounts.

1. What the app does

The app downloads clips from Twitch streamers who have given the operator permission, edits them into vertical videos, and uploads them to the TikTok and YouTube accounts that the operator connects to it.

2. Who can use it

The app is for the operator's personal use. If you sign in with TikTok, you authorize the app to upload videos only to your own account, and you must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

3. Content and permission

Clips belong to the streamers who created them. The app only processes clips from streamers who have agreed to have their clips reposted, and the operator keeps a record of that permission. If you are a rights holder and believe content was posted without permission, contact us using the details below and it will be removed.

4. Your TikTok account

You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Revoking access stops all further uploads.
